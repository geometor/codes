Bibliography¶

Primary treatises, foundational monographs, and software repositories audited in GEOMETOR Codes.

EuclidHeath1908¶

Heath, Thomas L., trans. The Thirteen Books of Euclid’s Elements. 3 vols. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1908 (2nd ed. 1926).

Byrne1847¶

Byrne, Oliver. The First Six Books of the Elements of Euclid in which Coloured Diagrams and Symbols are Used Instead of Letters for the Greater Ease of Learners. London: William Pickering (printed by Charles Whittingham, Chiswick Press), 1847.

Rougeux2018¶

Rougeux, Nicholas. Byrne’s Euclid: The First Six Books of the Elements of Euclid with Coloured Diagrams and Symbols. Interactive SVG/Web Edition, https://www.c82.net/euclid/, 2018.

Hilbert1899¶

Hilbert, David. Grundlagen der Geometrie. Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1899.

Lemoine1902¶

Lemoine, Émile. La Géométrographie ou l’art des constructions géométriques. Scientia #18. Paris: C. Naud, 1902.

Hoenen1954¶

Hoenen, Petrus H. J., S.J. De Noetica Geometriae: Origine Theoriae Cognitionis. Analecta Gregoriana #63. Rome: Pontificia Università Gregoriana, 1954.

Schwabhauser1983¶

Schwabhäuser, Wolfram, Wanda Szmielew, and Alfred Tarski. Metamathematische Methoden in der Geometrie. Berlin: Springer-Verlag, 1983.

ChouGaoZhang1994¶

Chou, Shang-Ching, Xiao-Shan Gao, and Jing-Zhong Zhang. Machine Proofs in Geometry: Automated Production of Readable Proofs for Geometry Theorems. Singapore: World Scientific, 1994.

Kimberling1998¶

Kimberling, Clark. Triangle Centers and Central Triangles. Congressus Numerantium #129 (1998), and Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers (ETC).

Wildberger2005¶

Wildberger, Norman J. Divine Proportions: Rational Trigonometry to Universal Geometry. Sydney: Wild Egg Books, 2005.

DorstFontijneMann2007¶

Dorst, Leo, Daniel Fontijne, and Stephen Mann. Geometric Algebra for Computer Science: An Object-Oriented Approach to Geometry. San Francisco: Morgan Kaufmann, 2007.

AvigadDeanMumma2009¶

Avigad, Jeremy, Edward Dean, and John Mumma. ‘A Formal System for Euclid’s Elements.’ The Review of Symbolic Logic 2, no. 4 (2009): 700–768.

YeChouGao2011¶

Ye, Zheng, Shang-Ching Chou, and Xiao-Shan Gao. ‘An Introduction to Java Geometry Expert.’ In Automated Deduction in Geometry (ADG 2008), LNCS 6301, 189–195. Berlin: Springer, 2011.

RichterGebert2011¶

Richter-Gebert, Jürgen. Perspectives on Projective Geometry: A Guided Tour Through Real and Complex Geometry. Berlin: Springer, 2011.

Boutry2019¶

Boutry, Pierre, Gabriel Braun, Cédric Coghetto, Julien Narboux, and Pascal Schreck. GeoCoq: A Formalization of Foundations of Geometry in Coq. https://geocoq.github.io/GeoCoq/, 2019–2025.

Gunn2020¶

Gunn, Charles, and Steven De Keninck. ‘Projective Geometric Algebra: A New Framework for Doing Euclidean Geometry.’ bivector.net & kingdon library, 2020–2025.

Mathlib2020¶

The mathlib Community. ‘The Lean Mathematical Library.’ Proceedings of the 9th ACM SIGPLAN International Conference on Certified Programs and Proofs (CPP 2020), 367–381.

Murphy2024¶

Murphy, Logan, Kaiyu Yang, Sunayana Rane, Robert Joseph George, and Vijay Ganesh. ‘Autoformalizing Euclidean Geometry (LeanEuclid).’ ICML 2024.

Trinh2024¶

Trinh, Trieu H., Yuhuai Wu, Quoc V. Le, He He, and Thang Luong. ‘Solving Olympiad Geometry Without Human Demonstrations (AlphaGeometry).’ Nature 625 (2024): 476–482.

Sicca2024¶

Sicca,Vladmir, et al. Newclid & Yuclid: A Modular Neuro-Symbolic Framework for Olympiad Geometry. https://github.com/LMCRC/Newclid, 2024–2025.
