============

*fl. 2011–2025* — **Pioneer of Projective Geometric Algebra (TU Berlin / bivector.net)**

Mathematician and computer scientist who pioneered modern 2D/3D Projective Geometric Algebra (PGA) for Euclidean and non-Euclidean geometry.
