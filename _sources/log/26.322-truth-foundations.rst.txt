===================================================
26.322 — Truth Foundations: Ladder, Stack, Graphs
===================================================

Regrouped CODES around one question — *why should I believe this is true?* —
and rebuilt the site around each system's **truth foundation** (``v0.3.0``):

- **Trust Stack & Truth Ladder**: Every system is described by the same seven
  layers (ontology, language, assumptions, engine, certificate, checker,
  semantics) and placed on a ladder of claim strength: L0 Plausible, L1
  Instance, L2 Generic, L3 Rule-closed, L4 Axiom-checked, L5 Foundational,
  plus H Human. Data lives in ``resources/foundations.toml``.
- **Graphs**: Truth ladder with lift routes, per-system trust chains, a
  who-trusts-what hub graph, bridges between systems, an ontology map, and a
  shared-principle overlap graph — all generated by ``geometor.codes.graphs``.
- **Showcase**: Five theorems realized side by side with commit-pinned source
  excerpts, each paired with an exact ``geometor.model`` witness (L1) and a
  symbolic-parameter **generic witness** (L2) computed at build time.
- **Audit findings**: LeanEuclid's ``euclid_finish`` closes goals through the
  ``SMT_VERIF`` axiom (rule-closed, not kernel-checked); AlphaGeometry and
  Newclid decide side conditions numerically; ``geometor.model``'s
  ``zero_test`` carries a high-precision numeric acceptance branch — a
  candidate *strict mode* for the model roadmap.
- **Presentation**: Interactive Atlas on the home page; the 667-principle
  catalog is now a single searchable appendix.
