Wildberger
==========

.. _wildberger:

Norman J. Wildberger (b. 1955)
------------------------------

Formulated Rational Trigonometry (*Divine Proportions*, 2005) and Chromogeometry, replacing irrational distance and transcendental angle with polynomial quadrance :math:`Q` and spread :math:`s`.
