26.321 — Principles Catalog & Cross-System Linker¶

Implemented the end-to-end GEOMETOR Principles Catalog (v0.2.0):

  • Canonical Lexicon: Seeded 127 concepts and 271 aliases from geometor.etc ops.py, with codes lexicon audit verifying alias uniqueness and installed SymPy resolution.

  • Base Context Mining: Extracted 1,425 declarations across Euclid (Heath), LeanEuclid (System E + Book I + UniGeo), AlphaGeometry, Newclid, sympy.geometry, and geometor.model into deterministic JSONL snapshots so codes runs standalone without _refs/.

  • Discovery & Linked Libraries: Normalized statements, computed relabeling-invariant fingerprints, clustered all 1,425 items into 667 candidate principles (107 multi-system, 0 unassigned), and indexed 8,931 declarations across GeoCoq, mathlib4, JGEX, kingdon, galgebra, and CindyJS.

  • Exact Witness Pilot: Verified 20 core principles in geometor.model with zero sp.Float coordinates (and 3 explicit order/betweenness gaps), rendering ETC-style entry pages and .euclid-graphic SVGs under docsrc/principles/.

Verification gates: pytest (19 passed), ruff check src tests demos (clean), and publish build (clean).
