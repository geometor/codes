1. Core Kernel Architecture (geometor.model)¶

Auditing all modules in model/src/geometor/model/ confirms how GEOMETOR avoids the traps that afflict generic CAS libraries and floating-point sketchers:

  1. Strict Zero-Float Gate (`model.py`): Model.__setitem__ inspects every coordinate expression and rejects float / sp.Float contamination at insertion time, guaranteeing 100% exact symbolic lineage across \(\mathbb{Q}\), \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{d})\), \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\), and nested quadratic towers.

  2. Targeted Ancestral DAG & Closed-Form Intersections (`intersections.py`): Setting auto_intersect=False replaces \(O(N^2)\) combinatorial intersection explosion with explicit model.intersect(el1, el2, ...) calls solved via closed-form radical-axis quadratic formulas—never invoking SymPy’s slow Circle.intersection / solve()—and folded cleanly via with model.subconstruction(...) as scope: scope.keep(...).

  3. Deterministic Topological Selectors: side_of=(p1, p2, ±1) (via exact signed_area2), between=(p1, p2), beyond=(p1, p2), on_ray=(p1, p2), and other_than=pt disambiguate quadratic roots without floating-point branch jumping.

  4. Canonical ``ANP`` Zero-Testing & Rational Trigonometry (`algebra.py`, `invariants.py`, `proof.py`): zero_test reduces nested radical expressions inside sp.QQ.algebraic_field via primitive_element, powering @proposition assertions (assert_equal, assert_spread_equal, assert_perpendicular, assert_parallel, assert_congruent, assert_area_equal) alongside polynomial quadrance, line_spread, vertex_spread, signed_area2, and cross_ratio.

2. Honest Self-Audit & Synthesis Roadmap¶

Comparing geometor.model (v0.6.0) against our 10 peer systems highlights three honest internal limitations—each directly addressed by our 11 Architectural Blueprints:

  1. High-Complexity Fallback in ``algebra.zero_test`` (`lines 247-251`): When count_ops(expr) > 25, zero_test skips primitive_element and falls back to abs(expr.evalf(80)) < 1e-65. Upgrading this to constructive BFMSS root-separation bounds (Blueprint 1) achieves 100% mathematical soundness at arbitrary tower depth.

  2. Step AST & Géométrographie Telemetry: Recording structured selector ASTs (Blueprint 2) and Émile Lemoine’s \((R_1, R_2, C_1, C_2, C_3)\) operational counts (Blueprint 3) on Step turns every Model into both a LeanEuclid proof generator and a quantitative complexity benchmark.

  3. First-Class Inversion, :math:`3 times 3` Conics & Area Elimination: Promoting geometor.model.inversion (Blueprint 4), ProjectiveConic \(3 \times 3\) symmetric matrices in geometor.pappus (Blueprint 5), and Chou–Gao–Zhang backward elimination in geometor.wildberger (Blueprint 6) completes a unified multi-lens engine unmatched by any single external system.
