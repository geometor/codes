Glossary¶

Foundational terms, logical paradigms, and algebraic invariants referenced across GEOMETOR Codes.

affine torsor¶

A point space \(P\) acted upon freely and transitively by a vector space \(V\) (NormedAddTorsor V P in mathlib4), allowing vector subtraction \(p_1 -_v p_2 \in V\) and vector translation \(v +_v p \in P\) without privileging an arbitrary coordinate origin \((0, 0)\).

Algebraic Number Polynomial¶

SymPy’s exact minimal-polynomial field element representation (ANP inside QQ.algebraic_field(ext)) used by geometor.model.algebra to canonicalize coordinates over \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\), \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{d})\), and radical towers without floating-point approximation.

Algebraic Reasoning¶

Sparse rational Gaussian elimination (ar.py in AlphaGeometry, SympyAR / Yuclid in Newclid) maintaining linear equivalence tables over directed angles modulo \(\pi\), logarithmic segment ratios, collinear distances, and (in Yuclid) squared lengths.

Area Method¶

The coordinate-free decision and readable-proof procedure of Chou, Gao, and Zhang (1994) that eliminates constructed points in reverse order from rational expressions in three geometric invariants: parallel signed ratios \(r\), signed areas \(S_{ABC}\), and Pythagoras differences \(P_{ABC}\).

chromatic value-token¶

An inline colored geometric glyph (segment, angle wedge, polygon, or circle in primary red, blue, yellow, or black) used directly inside algebraic-logical proof equations in Oliver Byrne’s 1847 Coloured Euclid, eliminating indirect vertex-letter pointer dereferencing.

circular points at infinity¶

The conjugate complex projective pair \(I = (1, i, 0)\) and \(J = (1, -i, 0)\) on the ideal line \(\ell_\infty = [0, 0, 1]\) in \(\mathbb{CP}^2\) (CindyJS), through which every Euclidean circle passes and relative to which Laguerre’s cross-ratio recovers Euclidean angle.

Conformal Geometric Algebra¶

The Minkowski Clifford algebra \(\mathbb{R}_{3,1}\) (Algebra(3, 1) in kingdon) embedding each planar point \((x, y)\) onto the 4D null cone \(P = x e_1 + y e_2 + \tfrac{1}{2}(x^2 + y^2)n_\infty + n_o\), unifying lines and circles so Steiner power is an inner product -2*(kappa | P), radical axis is kappa1 - kappa2, and circle inversion is a sandwich product C >> X.

Deductive Database¶

Forward-chaining Horn-clause saturation (GDD in JGEX, dd.py in AlphaGeometry) computing the finite relational fixed point of synthetic congruence, parallelism, perpendicularity, similarity, and concyclicity rules without branching search.

directed full-angle¶

An angle modulo \(\pi\) between ordered pairs of lines rather than rays—written eqangle in JGEX/AlphaGeometry/Newclid and \((2 : \mathbb{Z}) \bullet \measuredangle x y z\) in mathlib4—which eliminates acute/obtuse and supplementary case splits in cyclic quadrilateral proofs.

Five-Segment Configuration¶

Tarski’s angle-free replacement (FSC / outer_five_segment) for Euclid’s Side-Angle-Side congruence criterion (I.4), asserting that two collinear 4-tuples with matching base congruences transmit congruence to the fifth segment joining an apex.

Formal System E¶

The six-sorted constructive and diagrammatic formal system (Point, Line, Circle, Segment, Angle, Triangle) formulated by Jeremy Avigad, Edward Dean, and John Mumma (2009) and mechanized in LeanEuclid, separating explicit ruler-and-compass construction rules from diagrammatic plane-topology inferences and linear real metric transfer axioms.

Géométrographie¶

Émile Lemoine’s 1902 operational complexity calculus counting the five physical straightedge and compass primitives (\(R_1, R_2, C_1, C_2, C_3\)) of any geometric construction via its Simplicity \(S = \sum\) and Exactitude \(E = R_1 + C_1 + C_2\).

point-only ontology¶

Alfred Tarski’s minimalist first-order geometric signature (formalized in GeoCoq) in which the universe contains a single sort Tpoint and two primitive relations—ternary non-strict betweenness Bet A B C and quaternary segment equidistance Cong A B C D—eliminating lines, circles, and angles as primitive types.

Projective Geometric Algebra¶

The degenerate Clifford algebra \(\mathbb{R}_{2,0,1}^*\) (Algebra(2, 0, 1) in kingdon) whose basis vector \(e_0\) squares to \(0\), representing planar lines as grade-1 vectors, finite and ideal points uniformly as grade-2 bivectors, meets via ^, joins via &, and Euclidean isometries via versor sandwich products.

Pythagoras difference¶

The coordinate-free quadratic invariant \(P_{ABC} = |AB|^2 + |BC|^2 - |AC|^2\) (generalized to four points as \(P_{ABCD} = |AB|^2 + |CD|^2 - |BC|^2 - |DA|^2 = 2\overrightarrow{AC}\cdot\overrightarrow{BD}\)), which vanishes if and only if \(AB \perp BC\) (respectively \(AC \perp BD\)).

quadrance¶

Norman Wildberger’s polynomial squared separation \(Q(A, B) = (x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2\) in geometor.model and geometor.wildberger, avoiding irrational square roots so metric laws remain inside the base number field.

spread¶

Wildberger’s rational squared-sine invariant \(s(\ell_1, \ell_2) \in [0, 1]\) between two lines (\(0\) when parallel, \(3/4\) at \(60^\circ\), \(1\) when perpendicular), replacing transcendental arccos/atan2 angles with exact rational algebra.

versor sandwich product¶

The universal Clifford transformation law \(V \gg X = \pm V X \widetilde{V}\) applying reflections, rotations, translations, dilations, and conformal circle inversions uniformly across blades of every grade.
